# Research

- Scope each investigation: question, owner, evidence, and limits.
  Investigations specific to one project stay with that project.
- Separate observed findings, proposed repairs, and accepted decisions. Hash
  equality proves byte identity, not correctness or freshness. Tests and
  proposals do not approve adoption, external writes, or publication.
- Publish sanitised findings with provenance; keep raw captures in ignored
  `workbench/.local/`. Link accepted decisions to their owner.
- Keep material referenced by active investigations, decisions, or unresolved
  recovery work. Age alone does not authorise deletion.
